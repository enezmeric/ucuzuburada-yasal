# Delete Your Account

You can delete your Ucuzu Burada account at any time, free of charge. You do not need to reinstall the app.

## In the app

1. Open the **Profile** tab in the Ucuzu Burada app and tap the pencil on your profile card (**Edit profile**).
2. Tap **Delete Account** at the bottom of the page.
3. Confirm the deletion in the window that opens.

## On the website

1. Sign in to your account at ucuzuburada.com.
2. Choose **Delete Account** in the profile menu and confirm.

## If you cannot sign in

Send an e-mail with the subject "Account deletion request" to **kvkk@ucuzuburada.com**, from the e-mail address registered to your account or stating your phone number. We delete your account within 30 days after verifying your identity.

## What happens after deletion

- Your account is closed and can no longer be used to sign in. A deleted account cannot be restored.
- Your profile and all your ads are taken down. Your name appears as "Deleted user" in conversations.
- Your notification registrations and sessions are deleted.
- You can sign up again with the same e-mail address or phone number.

## Data we keep

As required by law, records of ads, messages, transactions, reports and security events are kept securely for the applicable legal periods, for possible disputes, fraud investigations and requests from the authorities. These records are not shown to other users. They are deleted or anonymised when those periods end. See the Privacy Policy for details.

## Approved Seller accounts

For Approved Seller accounts, the deletion request is sent to an administrator. Your account is deleted within 7 days at the latest and your Approved Seller badge is removed.
