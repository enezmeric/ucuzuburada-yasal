## 2. Definitions

- **Platform:** the ucuzuburada.com website, the Ucuzu Burada iOS and Android applications and all related sub-domains and services.
- **User / Member:** any natural or legal person who visits the Platform, registers, posts ads or responds to ads.
- **Individual Member:** a natural person who posts ads for their own goods, vehicles or property without a commercial purpose.
- **Approved Seller:** a User carrying on a commercial activity (dealer, real estate office, shop or similar) whose application has been reviewed and approved by the Operator and whose profile shows a gold badge. Approved Sellers are also bound by the Approved Seller Agreement.
- **Verified Profile:** a User whose phone number and e-mail address have been verified and whose profile shows a blue badge.
- **Ad:** the text, photos, videos and other content published by a User on the Platform about a product, vehicle, property, service or job.
- **Story:** content in the story area at the top of the Platform's home screen, made of the photos and videos of featured ads and the Operator's announcements.
- **Paid Services:** the Featured (showcase and home story) and Urgent services that increase the visibility of ads, and similar services the Operator may offer in the future.
- **Credit:** a unit, bought singly or in packs in the app and never expiring, that runs a Paid Service on one ad for 24 hours.
