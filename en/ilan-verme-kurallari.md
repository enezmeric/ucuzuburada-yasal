# Listing Rules

_Last updated: 16 October 2026_

These rules are an annex to the User Agreement. Our aim is a trustworthy marketplace where buyers find accurate information quickly and sellers compete fairly. Ads go live without waiting for approval; ads that break these rules may be edited, taken into review or removed without notice.

## 1. General Rules

1. Each ad must relate to a single product, vehicle, property, service or job. Separate items require separate ads. Several identical items (e.g. stock of the same model) may be listed in one ad with the quantity stated.
2. Duplicate ads for the same item are not allowed, nor is deleting and re-posting an ad to push it up the lists. Use the free "Bump up" feature to move your ad to the top instead.
3. Ads must be posted in the correct category and at the item's actual location.
4. The advertiser must be entitled to sell, rent or offer the item. Ads may not be posted on behalf of others; Approved Sellers may only list items belonging to their own business.
5. Ads for items that have been sold, rented or are no longer available must be removed within 3 days.
6. No ads may be posted for anything on the Prohibited Items and Services List.

## 2. Title and Description

1. The title must describe the item clearly and accurately. Titles may not contain "URGENT", "BARGAIN", "!!!", excessive capitals, phone numbers, prices or unrelated words (the "Urgent" badge can only be added with an Urgent credit in the app).
2. The description may only contain genuine information about the item. Defects, damage and missing parts must be stated clearly.
3. Descriptions may not direct users to other ads, websites, social media accounts or messaging apps, and may not contain e-mail addresses or IBANs. Phone numbers, e-mail addresses, IBANs, links and social media handles are hidden automatically in the title and description; buyers reach you through in-app messages or the numbers you choose to show on the ad.
4. Political, religious, discriminatory, obscene, insulting or unrelated statements are not allowed.
5. Brand names may only be used to state the item's real brand, not to attract searches (e.g. "like an iPhone").
6. Category-specific fields (year, mileage, rooms, title deed type etc.) must be filled in correctly.

## 3. Price

1. The price must be the item's genuine sale or rental price. Symbolic or unrealistic prices intended to mislead (such as 1 TL or 111 TL) are not allowed.
2. The currency selected (TL, GBP, EUR, USD) must be correct. Rental ads must state in the description whether the price is monthly, daily or for another period.
3. Instalments, down payments, deposits and additional costs (VAT, transformer fee, title deed fees, commission etc.) must be stated in the description.
4. No fee of any kind may be requested in free adoption ads.

## 4. Photos and Videos

1. Images must be of the actual item, current, sharp and well lit. Images taken from other ads, the internet or catalogues may not be used (for new items, manufacturer images may be used together with a photo of the item itself).
2. Images may not contain phone numbers, web addresses or logos or watermarks of other platforms.
3. Recognisable faces, children, number plates or identity details must be hidden.
4. Obscene, violent or disturbing images are not allowed.
5. A video (Reel) added to an ad may only show the advertised item. Video length and size are limited as shown in the app.

## 5. Category-Specific Rules

### 5.1 Vehicles

- Vehicle details (make, model, year, mileage, fuel, gearbox, steering side etc.) must match the registration documents.
- Damage, paint and replaced-part history, and for imported vehicles the origin and customs status, must be stated clearly.
- Vehicles with altered (rolled-back) mileage may not be advertised.
- Spare part ads must state whether the part is new, used or salvaged.
- Rental vehicle ads must state the rental terms (minimum period, deposit, driver age, insurance) and the advertiser must hold the required permits.

### 5.2 Real Estate

- The title deed type (Turkish title, equivalent, allocation, foreign title etc.) must be stated correctly. If the title status is unknown, this must be stated.
- Sale ads must state who bears VAT, the transformer fee and title transfer costs.
- Rental ads must state the payment period (monthly, 6 months or 1 year in advance), deposit, service charges and whether bills are included.
- Off-plan properties must include the completion date and building permit information.
- Real estate offices may only advertise properties they are authorised to market.

### 5.3 Jobs

- Job ads must relate to a real position and identify the employer or recruiter.
- Applicants may **not be charged** any fee for applications, training, work permits, visas or similar.
- Discriminatory statements based on gender, age, religion, race, ethnic origin or disability are not allowed (except for lawful exceptions required by the nature of the job).
- Employers stating that a work permit will be provided must act in accordance with TRNC legislation.
- Work-from-home schemes, pyramid schemes or ads promising returns in exchange for an investment are not allowed.

### 5.4 Animals

- The age, health and vaccination status of animals must be stated correctly. Young animals below the age at which they may be separated from their mother may not be advertised.
- Protected, wild or species whose import or sale is prohibited may not be advertised.
- No fee, "food money" or similar charge may be requested in free adoption ads.

### 5.5 Services and Private Tutors

- Services requiring a professional qualification or licence (electrical installation, health services, transport etc.) may only be advertised by qualified persons.
- Ads for exam questions, homework or thesis writing or cheating services are not allowed.

## 6. Review and Sanctions

1. Every ad passes automatic (AI-assisted) checks when it goes live and whenever its text or photos change: profanity and insults, disguised contact details, signs of fraud, meaningless or repeated content, prohibited items, inappropriate photos and photos already used on another account. An ad flagged by these checks is taken offline for review by our team.
2. Ads may also be reviewed by moderators following user reports. An ad reported by a certain number of different users may be temporarily removed from lists until the review is complete.
3. The Operator may remove an ad that breaks the rules without giving reasons. If a featured or urgent ad is taken into review or removed, the unused full days are returned to the owner's account as credits.
4. Possible sanctions include: requesting a correction, taking the ad into review or removing it, stopping paid services, temporarily restricting the account and, for serious or repeated breaches, permanently closing the account.
5. Ads considered unlawful and related records may be reported to the competent authorities.
6. If you see an ad that breaks the rules, use the "Report" option on the ad page or write to hukuk@ucuzuburada.com.
