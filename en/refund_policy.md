# Refund and Withdrawal Policy

_Last updated: 16 October 2026_

## Posting ads is free

Posting ads and bumping them up on Ucuzu Burada is free within the limits set for your profile type. This policy applies only to the Featured and Urgent credits (singly or in packs) bought in the app.

## Right of withdrawal

Credits are digital content added to your account the moment you buy them. Because you confirm at purchase that delivery should happen straight away, the right of withdrawal cannot be exercised once the credits are on your account. Credits on your account never expire; no unused credit is lost.

## Store refunds

- Purchases in the app are made through the Apple App Store or Google Play, and the store decides on refunds. You can send your request directly to the store: for Apple at _reportaproblem.apple.com_, for Google Play under _Payments & subscriptions > Budget & history_ in the Play Store app.
- When a store refunds a purchase, the unused credits that purchase added to your account are taken back. Credits used before the refund cannot be taken back.

## Days returned automatically

If an ad you featured or marked urgent is taken into review or removed by the automatic checks or by our team, the service stops and the unused full days (half a day or more counts as a full day) are returned to your account as credits automatically. You do not need to apply for this.

## When do we compensate?

- If, because of a technical problem on our side, credits were not added to your account or a service you used did not work for a significant part of its period, we restore your credits or extend the period.
- If we find that you were charged twice for the same purchase, we help you get the extra amount refunded by the store.

## When is there no refund?

- When you cannot use the remaining time because you removed the ad yourself, marked it as sold or deleted your account;
- When the service did not bring the views, messages or sale you expected;
- For gift credits granted free of charge as part of a campaign or cooperation.

## How to reach us

For compensation for technical problems, write to destek@ucuzuburada.com stating the purchase date, the store receipt and the ad concerned. Requests are resolved within 7 business days at the latest.

## Banner advertising

Banner ads on the home screen and category pages are not sold in the app; their payment and refund terms are set out in the Advertising Services Agreement signed between the parties. Contact: reklam@ucuzuburada.com
