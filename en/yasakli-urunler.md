# Prohibited Items and Services

_Last updated: 16 October 2026_

This list is an annex to the User Agreement. The items and services below may not be advertised on the Platform, promoted as stories or advertising, or traded through in-app messaging. The list is illustrative: anything whose sale, possession or advertising is prohibited or requires a licence under TRNC law is prohibited even if not listed. If in doubt, do not post.

## 1. Drugs, Medicines and Health Products

- Narcotics and stimulants, their derivatives and paraphernalia; cannabis and cannabis-containing products;
- Prescription or over-the-counter medicines, hormones, steroids, slimming and sexual performance products;
- Used or unlicensed medical devices, contact lenses and other products requiring a prescription;
- Blood, organs, tissue, breast milk and other products of human origin.

## 2. Tobacco, Alcohol and Addictive Products

- Cigarettes, cigars, tobacco, hookahs and hookah tobacco, e-cigarettes, heated tobacco products and their liquids and cartridges;
- Alcoholic beverages (corporate promotions by licensed businesses only, subject to the Operator's separate approval).

## 3. Weapons, Ammunition and Dangerous Goods

- Firearms, their parts, cartridges and ammunition; air rifles and pistols; blank-firing guns and replica weapons;
- Knives, daggers, switchblades, knuckle-dusters, stun guns, pepper spray and other offensive or defensive weapons (kitchen and hobby knives excepted);
- Fireworks, explosives, flammable, toxic and radioactive substances;
- Banned pesticides and chemicals.

## 4. Crime and Security

- Stolen, smuggled, untaxed or goods of unknown origin;
- Counterfeit or imitation goods, fake brand products and replicas;
- Identity cards, passports, driving licences, registration documents, title deeds, diplomas, number plates and other official documents, fakes of them or blank documents;
- Credit cards, bank accounts, SIM cards and lines, social media or gaming accounts belonging to others or whose transfer is prohibited;
- Personal data lists, subscriber or customer databases;
- Covert listening and recording devices, spyware, signal jammers, lock-picking and burglary tools;
- Uniforms, badges and equipment of the police, army and other public officials;
- Counterfeit money and materials for producing it.

## 5. Cultural Heritage and Nature

- **Antiquities, historical objects, archaeological finds, ancient coins, icons from churches and monasteries** and other protected cultural property;
- Protected plant and animal species and products derived from them (ivory, coral, sea turtle products, Cyprus mouflon and similar);
- Prohibited hunting methods and equipment, and game out of season.

## 6. Finance, Gambling and Games of Chance

- Betting, gambling, games of chance and lottery services, and ads directing users to them;
- Loans, lending, usury, bill discounting and unlicensed financial advice;
- Ads promising returns through cryptocurrency, investment, forex or similar; pyramid and Ponzi schemes;
- Resale of gift vouchers, lottery tickets and similar instruments.

## 7. Adult Content and Personal Rights

- Obscene content, sexual products and services, escort and similar services;
- Hate speech, violence, and symbols or materials associated with terrorist organisations;
- Photos, videos or personal information shared without the consent of the persons concerned.

## 8. Intellectual Property Infringements

- Pirated films, music, software, games, digital content and copies of them;
- Licence keys, shared accounts and transfers of memberships;
- Products using others' designs, trademarks or logos without permission.

## 9. Other Prohibited Ads

- Human trafficking, marriage brokerage, surrogacy and similar services;
- Job ads charging for applications, training or work permits;
- Exam questions, homework, thesis and fake report services;
- Ads by persons without the required licence for goods whose sale requires one (fuel, food supplements, veterinary medicines etc.);
- Ads that are purely advertising and do not relate to a specific item or service;
- Any item or service that, in the Operator's reasonable opinion, endangers the safety of Users or the reputation of the Platform.

## Sanctions

Ads that breach this list are removed immediately; the account may be restricted or closed, and unused credits on closed accounts are not refunded. Ads considered to constitute an offence and related records are forwarded to the competent authorities. If you see a prohibited ad, use the "Report" option or write to hukuk@ucuzuburada.com.
