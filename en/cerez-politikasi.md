# Cookie Policy

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies used on the ucuzuburada.com website and in the Ucuzu Burada mobile applications.

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files saved in your browser when you visit a website. In mobile apps, local data stored on the device and device identifiers are used for the same purpose.

## 2. Cookies we use

| Type | Purpose | Examples | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Strictly necessary** | Keeping you signed in, remembering your language and location, consistent list ordering, security | Session key, language, location, list ordering key, cookie preference | Session or up to 1 year |
| **Analytics** (consent-based) | Understanding how the site is used, fixing errors and improving the experience | Google Firebase Analytics, Microsoft Clarity | As set by the provider, up to 2 years |

Strictly necessary cookies are required for the site to work and are not subject to consent. Analytics cookies are used only if you accept them in the cookie banner. The site uses no advertising cookies.

Our mobile apps use no advertising or tracking tools. To understand how the app is used and to fix errors, the app collects usage statistics that do not identify you, with Google Firebase Analytics (for example which screens are opened and which features are used). These statistics are not linked to your name, phone number, e-mail, messages or account; no advertising ID is used and the statistics are not used to personalise ads. Usage statistics are collected from every installation of the app; you can send objections and deletion requests to kvkk@ucuzuburada.com. Apart from that, the app stores on your device only the data it needs to work (session, language and location preference, list ordering key). The app shows no cookie consent window. Your device asks separately for permission to use location, notifications, the camera and photos.

## 3. Managing your preferences

- On the website you can accept or reject cookies in the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time from the “Cookie preferences” link at the bottom of the page and on the Legal information page; when you withdraw your consent, the analytics cookies are deleted from your device.
- You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some parts of the site may not work properly as a result.

## 4. Third-party providers

Analytics cookies are subject to the providers' own privacy policies: Google (policies.google.com/privacy), Microsoft (privacy.microsoft.com).

## 5. Contact

For questions: kvkk@ucuzuburada.com
