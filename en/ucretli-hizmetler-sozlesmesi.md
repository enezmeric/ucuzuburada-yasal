# Featured and Urgent Services Agreement

_Last updated: 16 October 2026_

## 1. Parties and Subject

This Agreement is made between [personal detail; on the site] (the "Operator") and the User who buys or uses Featured or Urgent credits on the Platform (the "Buyer"), and sets out the scope of these services, the terms of use and the rights and obligations of the parties. The User Agreement, the Listing Rules, the Distance Sales Agreement and the Refund and Withdrawal Policy form an integral part of this Agreement.

## 2. Services

Using the Platform, posting ads and bumping an ad up are free. There are two paid services that increase an ad's visibility:

- **Featured:** for as long as credits run, the ad is shown in the showcase (the places reserved for featured ads on the home screen and in lists, marked "Featured") and in the story area at the top of the home screen. The story is made of the ad's own photos and, if any, its video.
- **Urgent:** for as long as credits run, the ad carries a red "URGENT" badge and appears in urgent-ad searches.

## 3. Credits

1. The services are sold as "credits". Each credit runs the service on one ad for 24 hours. In one go, credits may be used on one or more ads, from 1 to 30 days each; adding credits to a running service extends it.
2. Credits are sold singly, in packs of 5 and 10, or by subscription (section 12); contents and price are shown on the purchase screen.
3. Credits bought singly or in packs never expire, stay on the account until used and can be used in the Android and iPhone apps when signed in with the same account. Credits cannot be transferred to another account or exchanged for cash.
4. Credits can be used only on the Buyer's own live ads.

## 4. Campaigns and Gift Credits

1. The Operator may run campaigns open to everyone or joined with a code; packs bought during a campaign receive gift credits at the announced rate. The rate, duration and any usage limit of a campaign are shown on the purchase screen.
2. The Operator may grant gift credits to Approved Sellers or to businesses it cooperates with.
3. Gift credits are used in the same way as purchased credits; as they are given free of charge, they are not refundable.

## 5. Display Principles

1. Featured ads are shown in rotation with the other featured ads sharing the same places. The Operator does not promise any particular position, place, number of views, clicks or sale.
2. A featured ad is shown only in lists that match its category and the filters chosen by the user (location, price, features, etc.).
3. The Operator may change the placement, frequency, visual markers and ordering method of the display areas without changing the essence of the service. The ad owner may be shown how many times their story was watched.
4. Featured ads may be shared on the Operator's social media accounts for promotion. A Buyer who does not want this can turn off the "My featured ads may be shared on social media" setting in Edit profile.

## 6. Purchase and Payment

1. In the mobile applications credits are sold only through the Apple App Store or Google Play in-app purchase systems. Payment, billing and store refunds are subject to the terms of the store concerned. The Operator has no access to the Buyer's card details.
2. Credits are added to the Buyer's account immediately after the store confirms the payment and the Operator's server verifies it.
3. Prices include taxes; the currency and amount shown may vary with the store's country.

## 7. Content Compliance and Return of Unused Days

1. Ads using the services must comply with the User Agreement, the Listing Rules and the Prohibited Items and Services List.
2. If an ad with a running service is taken into review or removed by the automatic checks or by the Operator, the service stops and the remaining full days are returned to the Buyer's account as credits; the day under way counts as used.
3. If the Buyer marks the ad as sold, the service stops and the remaining full days are returned to their account as credits; the day it is marked counts as used (for example, an ad sold on the first day of a 5-day feature gets 4 days back). If the Buyer removes the ad or deletes their account, the remaining time is not refunded and cannot be moved to another ad.

## 8. Right of Withdrawal and Refunds

1. Credits are digital content added to the account at the moment of purchase. The Buyer accepts that they consented, at the purchase step, to performance starting immediately and therefore has no right of withdrawal.
2. When a store refunds a purchase, the credits from that purchase still on the account are taken back; credits already used cannot be taken back.
3. If, because of a technical fault on the Operator's side, a service was not provided at all or for a significant part of the purchased period, the Operator restores the credits or extends the period. Such requests are sent to destek@ucuzuburada.com.
4. Details are in the Refund and Withdrawal Policy.

## 9. Misuse

If the services are used to mislead other Users (for example permanently marking a sale that is not urgent as "URGENT"), or an unfair advantage is obtained through multiple accounts, automated tools or abuse of store refund processes, the Operator may end the service without refund, take back credits added unfairly and take action against the account.

## 10. Liability

The Operator does not guarantee that a service will result in a sale, rental or contact. Brief unavailability of the Platform for maintenance, updates or force majeure does not mean the service was not performed. The Operator's liability is in all cases limited to the price paid for the credits concerned.

## 11. Banner Advertising

Banner advertising spaces on the home screen and category pages are not covered by this Agreement and are not sold in the app. They are business advertising services arranged through reklam@ucuzuburada.com and governed by a separate written agreement.

## 12. Subscriptions and Timed Credits

1. With a subscription, on every day it runs (Cyprus time) the chosen plan's Featured and Urgent credits are added to the Buyer's account. On Pro and higher plans the Buyer's live ads are also moved up once a day.
2. Each day's subscription credits are valid for 45 days from when they are added; those not used by then expire. Credits the Operator gives as a gift with a stated period must also be used within that period.
3. When credits are used, the one that expires soonest is used first and credits that never expire are used last.
4. Subscriptions are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play and renew automatically at the same price unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. They are managed and cancelled in the subscriptions section of the store account; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period. Credits already added stay usable for their own validity after the subscription ends.
5. If the store refunds a subscription, its unused credits are removed and running services are shortened by the credits used.
6. The Operator may give a user a free subscription for a stated period; it ends by itself and does not renew.

## 13. Changes, Governing Law and Jurisdiction

The Operator may change the scope of the services, the packs and the prices for the future; credits already on an account are preserved. This Agreement is governed by TRNC law; the Courts and Enforcement Offices of Lefkoşa (Nicosia) have jurisdiction over disputes. Statutory consumer rights are reserved.
