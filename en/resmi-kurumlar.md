# Guidelines for Authorities

This page explains how judicial and administrative authorities can submit requests for information about Ucuzu Burada users.

## Who can submit requests?

Courts, the Attorney General's Office, the Police General Directorate and other public bodies legally authorised to request information.

## How to submit a request

- Requests must be sent as an official letter, from an institutional e-mail address to **hukuk@ucuzuburada.com**, or to [personal detail; on the site].
- The request should state the requesting authority and the responsible official, the legal basis or decision, identifying information such as the ad number, ad link, user name, phone number or e-mail address, and the scope and date range of the information requested.

## Review

- Requests are reviewed for compliance with TRNC legislation and for scope. Duly issued requests are answered as quickly as possible.
- Users' personal data are shared only to the extent required by the request.
- Emergencies involving risk to life are given priority; please add "URGENT" to the e-mail subject for such requests.

## Reporting unlawful content

Authorities may request the removal of ads they consider unlawful by writing to hukuk@ucuzuburada.com.
