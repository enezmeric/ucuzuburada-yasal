## 4. Method of Collection

Personal data are collected electronically: directly from you through the Platform's registration, ad, messaging, purchase and application forms; from your Google or Apple account when you sign in with it; and automatically through cookies, usage statistics in the app and device permissions.

## 5. Transfers

Personal data may be transferred, limited to the purposes above, to the suppliers from whom we obtain hosting, authentication, notification, translation, AI-assisted content checks, mapping, analytics and e-mail/SMS services (Google, Apple, Microsoft and similar providers whose servers may be located outside the TRNC), to Apple and Google for in-app purchases, to other users you contact, and to competent public authorities.

## 6. Retention Period

Personal data are kept for as long as required by the purpose of processing and for the retention and limitation periods set by the relevant legislation, and are then deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

## 7. Rights of the Data Subject

As a data subject, you have the right to apply to the Operator to:

- learn whether your personal data are processed and, if so, request information about it;
- learn the purpose of processing and whether data are used in line with it;
- know the third parties in the TRNC or abroad to whom data are transferred;
- request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data;
- request deletion or destruction of data once the reasons for processing no longer exist;
- request that corrections, deletions and destructions be notified to third parties to whom data were transferred;
- object to a result against you arising exclusively from automated analysis of your data;
- claim compensation for damage suffered due to unlawful processing.

You may withdraw your consent to processing based on explicit consent at any time.

## 8. How to Apply

Send your request, with information that allows us to verify your identity (name, and the e-mail or phone number registered on the Platform), from your registered e-mail address to kvkk@ucuzuburada.com or in writing to [personal detail; on the site]. Requests are resolved free of charge as soon as possible and within 30 days at the latest, depending on their nature; if the action requires additional cost, the fee provided for by legislation may be charged.
