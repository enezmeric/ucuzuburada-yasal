# Safe Trading Guide

These precautions help protect you against fraudsters. Remember: Ucuzu Burada is not a party to transactions; it does not take payments or act as a payment intermediary.

## Basic rules

- **Never send money before seeing the item.** Be very careful with ads asking for a deposit, advance payment, "reservation fee" or "shipping fee".
- **Keep conversations in the app.** People who move you to WhatsApp, Telegram or e-mail may be trying to avoid leaving a trace.
- **Never share verification codes, passwords or card details.** Ucuzu Burada staff will never ask you for a password, SMS code or payment.
- **Be suspicious of unrealistic prices.** Prices far below market value are the most common fraud tactic.
- **Check the seller's profile.** Look at the badge, member since date, other ads and reviews.
- **Do not click links.** Messages such as "Your payment is confirmed, click to receive it" are designed to steal your card details.

## Meeting and handover

- Meet in daylight in busy places with cameras (shopping centres, petrol stations, near a police station).
- If you need to go to a home for large items, take someone with you.
- Test the item and compare it with the ad before accepting it.
- For cash payments check that the money is genuine; for large amounts prefer to complete the transaction at a bank branch.

## Buying a vehicle

- Check that the registration belongs to the seller and that the chassis and engine numbers match the registration.
- Have the relevant office check for debts, seizures or liens on the vehicle.
- For imported vehicles ask for customs and inspection documents; confirm the steering side and origin.
- Have a mechanic you trust inspect it; research the mileage and damage history.
- Do not pay the full price before the transfer is completed; complete the transfer together at the official office.

## Buying or renting a home

- Before buying, verify the title deed type (Turkish title, equivalent, allocation, foreign title) and that the title belongs to the seller with the Land Registry.
- Check for mortgages, seizures or annotations on the property; get help from a lawyer if possible.
- Agree in writing who pays VAT, the transformer fee, title deed fees and other costs.
- When renting, do not send a deposit or advance rent before seeing the property; sign a written tenancy agreement and keep proof of payments.
- Be careful with landlords who say "I am abroad, I will post you the keys".

## For sellers

- Do not hand over the item before receiving payment; for bank transfers confirm with your bank that the money has actually arrived.
- "I overpaid, please refund the difference" is a classic scam.
- Never share a copy of your ID, a picture of your bank card or SMS codes.
- Do not go on a test drive alone; see the buyer's driving licence.

## Job ads

- Be careful with employers who ask you to pay for applications, training, work permits or visas; such ads are prohibited on the Platform.
- Hold interviews at the workplace and share your identity details only once the hiring is confirmed.

## If something goes wrong

1. Report the ad or profile with the "Report" option and block the user.
2. If you think you have been defrauded, go to the nearest police station without delay. We share our records upon request from the competent authorities.
3. You can reach us at destek@ucuzuburada.com or hukuk@ucuzuburada.com.
