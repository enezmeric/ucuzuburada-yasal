# Contact Us

| --- | --- |
| **Account, ad and technical support** | destek@ucuzuburada.com |
| **Home and category banner advertising** | reklam@ucuzuburada.com |
| **Personal data requests** | kvkk@ucuzuburada.com |
| **Unlawful content reports, requests from authorities** | hukuk@ucuzuburada.com |

## Questions about ads

For questions about an ad, contact the advertiser through in-app messaging. If you see an ad that breaks the rules, the "Report" option on the ad page is the fastest way to have it reviewed.

## Security warning

The Ucuzu Burada team will never ask for your password, SMS verification code, card details or any payment. Please report such requests from people claiming to work for Ucuzu Burada to [hukuk@ucuzuburada.com](mailto:hukuk@ucuzuburada.com).
