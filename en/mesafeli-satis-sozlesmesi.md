# Distance Sales Agreement

_Last updated: 16 October 2026_

## 1. Parties

**Seller (Service Provider):** [personal detail; on the site], [personal detail; on the site]. E-mail: destek@ucuzuburada.com

**Buyer:** the User who buys Featured or Urgent credits in the Platform's mobile applications and whose details are on file in their account.

## 2. Subject

This Agreement sets out the rights and obligations of the parties regarding the sale and performance of the digital credits (Featured and Urgent credits, singly or in packs) bought electronically by the Buyer in the Platform's mobile applications, within the framework of the consumer protection legislation in force in the TRNC.

## 3. Main Features and Price of the Service

Each credit runs the service concerned for 24 hours on an ad chosen by the Buyer: Featured shows the ad in the showcase and the home stories, Urgent shows it with a red "URGENT" badge. The contents of the pack bought (type and number of credits, and any campaign gift) and the total price including taxes are shown to the Buyer on the purchase screen and in the store's payment sheet before the payment is confirmed. How the services work is explained in the Featured and Urgent Services Agreement.

## 4. Payment

Payment is collected through the Apple App Store or Google Play in-app purchase system, using the payment method set up in the store concerned. The purchase record and receipt are kept in the store account through which the payment was made. Payment processing and payment security are the responsibility of the store; the Seller does not see or store the Buyer's card details.

## 5. Performance and Delivery

The credits are added to the Buyer's account immediately once the store confirms the payment and the Seller's server verifies it; adding the credits to the account constitutes delivery. Credits never expire; the Buyer uses them on their own live ads whenever they wish. If, because of a technical problem, the credits are not added within 24 hours, the Buyer may contact destek@ucuzuburada.com with the store receipt.

## 6. Right of Withdrawal

The credits bought are digital content delivered electronically at once. The Buyer accepts that, at the purchase step, they asked for performance to start immediately, before the withdrawal period ends, and that they lose the right of withdrawal on delivery. The stores' own refund processes and the refund conditions are explained in the Refund and Withdrawal Policy.

## 7. Obligations of the Parties

- The Seller must add the credits of the type and number stated on the purchase screen to the account and provide the service on the ad they are used on for the stated period.
- The Buyer is responsible for the compliance with the Platform rules and the law of the ads they use credits on. For ads taken into review or removed for breaching the rules, the unused full days are returned to the Buyer's account as credits.

## 8. Complaints and Disputes

The Buyer may send complaints to destek@ucuzuburada.com; they are answered within 15 days at the latest. Disputes arising from this Agreement are governed by TRNC law; without prejudice to the Buyer's rights of application under consumer law, the Courts and Enforcement Offices of Lefkoşa (Nicosia) have jurisdiction.

## 9. Entry into Force

Before confirming the purchase, the Buyer declares that they have read, understood and electronically accepted this Agreement and the preliminary information. The Agreement enters into force when the purchase is confirmed.
