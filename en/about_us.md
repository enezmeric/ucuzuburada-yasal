# About Us

**Ucuzu Burada is a free, Turkish- and English-language classifieds app and website for second-hand and new goods, cars, property and jobs in North Cyprus (TRNC).** It brings buyers and sellers together from all six districts of the island, from Nicosia to Kyrenia, from Famagusta to Iskele, Morphou and Lefka.

## At a glance

- **Founded:** 2026, North Cyprus.
- **Area served:** the six districts of North Cyprus: Lefkoşa (Nicosia), Girne (Kyrenia), Gazimağusa (Famagusta), İskele (Trikomo), Güzelyurt (Morphou) and Lefke (Lefka).
- **Free ads:** 5 live ads on a regular profile, 30 on a blue tick profile, unlimited for approved (gold tick) businesses.
- **Messaging without your number:** chat and get offers in the app without sharing your phone number.
- **Trust badges:** a blue tick for profiles with a verified phone and e-mail, a gold tick for businesses whose application has been reviewed and approved.
- **Prices:** in TL, GBP, EUR and USD; search compares them at current exchange rates.
- **Two languages:** Turkish and English; ads are translated automatically and reach international buyers on the island too.
- **Apps:** on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android; you can also post ads and chat on the website.
- **Payments:** posting ads is free. Optional extras such as Featured and Urgent are bought only inside the app (App Store / Google Play); nothing is sold on the website.

## How it works

1. **Take photos, post your ad.** AI suggests a title and category from the photos; your ad goes live without waiting.
2. **Chat.** Buyers message you and make offers in the app; your number shows on the ad only if you choose it.
3. **Meet and complete the sale.** See the item first and hand it over in a busy, safe place; once sold, mark your ad as "Sold".

## Who it's for

- **People living in North Cyprus:** anyone selling what they no longer need or looking for a used car, a home to rent or buy, a job or a tradesperson.
- **Newcomers from Turkey and students:** affordable furniture for a new home, flats to rent, private tutors and jobs; and a quick way to sell everything when leaving the island.
- **International residents:** an English interface, automatically translated ads and one-tap translation in chat mean you can buy and sell without speaking Turkish.
- **Businesses:** car dealers, estate agents, shops and tradespeople; the gold tick brings unlimited ads, your own short address (ucuzuburada.com/yourname) and more than one phone number on your account.

## In numbers

- 8 main categories, 972 categories in all with their subcategories.
- 6 districts, 2 languages, 4 currencies.

## A safe marketplace

Ads go live without waiting; their text and photos are scanned by AI in the background, and an ad showing fraud, a prohibited item or inappropriate content is taken into review. Phone numbers, e-mails and links typed into an ad are hidden automatically. Every ad and chat has report and block buttons.

## Please note

Ucuzu Burada is a hosting provider that brings buyers and sellers together; it is not the seller, buyer or agent of the products and services advertised, and it takes no payments. For safe trading, please read our Safe Trading Guide.
