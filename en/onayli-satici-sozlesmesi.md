# Approved Seller (Business Membership) Agreement

_Last updated: 16 October 2026_

## 1. Parties and Scope

This Agreement is made between [personal detail; on the site] (the "Operator") and the natural or legal person who applies for, and is granted, Approved Seller status in order to carry on a commercial activity on the Platform (the "Approved Seller"). The User Agreement and its annexes apply to Approved Sellers in full; this Agreement sets out additional rights and obligations. It is accepted electronically by ticking the consent box on the application form.

## 2. A Free Service

Approved Seller status is free of charge and will remain free. No fee, subscription or commission is charged, under any name, for applying, for the review, for approval or for keeping the status. Please report anyone who introduces themselves on behalf of the Operator and asks for payment for this status to hukuk@ucuzuburada.com.

Like any other User, Approved Sellers may choose to buy Featured and Urgent credits in the app; these credits are not a condition of Approved Seller status.

## 3. Application

1. Applications are made in the app or on the website from accounts whose phone number and e-mail address are verified (blue badge).
2. The application asks for the business's category, the full name of the authorised person, the business name, a contact phone number and the business address; a website may be added if there is one. Where it sees fit, the Operator may ask for further information and documents such as a trade registry or tradesman record, tax certificate or professional licence.
3. The applicant confirms that the information given is accurate, up to date and belongs to the business, and that they are authorised to apply on its behalf. Changes are notified to the Operator within 15 days.
4. After reviewing the application the Operator approves it or declines it with its reasons. A declined application may be renewed once the shortcomings are remedied.

## 4. Getting to Know Each Other, Visits and Contact

1. The Approved Seller agrees that, while the application is reviewed and while the status continues, the Operator or its authorised staff may contact them on the phone numbers and e-mail address given in the application.
2. To get to know the business and verify its details, the Operator may visit the premises in person and meet its representatives face to face, with prior notice and at a time convenient for the Approved Seller.
3. In such meetings the Operator may propose cooperation on matters such as using the Platform, campaigns, promotion and advertising. Such proposals are not binding; the Approved Seller is free to accept them, and declining does not affect their status.
4. The Approved Seller may tell destek@ucuzuburada.com which topics they do not wish to be contacted about and which channel they prefer.

## 5. Rights of the Approved Seller

- A gold badge and the words "Approved Seller" on their profile and ads;
- A higher or unlimited number of active ads, and more photos per ad, within the limits set by the Operator;
- A short profile address (ucuzuburada.com/businessname);
- Several verified contact numbers on the account, choosing for each ad which numbers are shown;
- Bumping ads up for free more often;
- Gift Featured and Urgent credits the Operator may grant as part of a campaign or business cooperation.

The scope and limits of these rights are shown on the Platform; the Operator may change them with prior notice.

## 6. Obligations of the Approved Seller

- To post ads only for products and properties that belong to their own business or that they are authorised to sell or rent;
- To carry on their business in line with TRNC law, in particular consumer protection, tax, trade, vehicle and property sales and estate agency rules, and to hold the necessary permits, licences and registrations;
- To fulfil the warranty, defective goods, invoice/receipt and information obligations they owe to consumers as a seller or service provider;
- To show prices, additional costs, commissions, VAT and title deed/registration details clearly and accurately in their ads;
- To let only authorised employees use the account and to be responsible for their actions on the Platform;
- To use user data obtained through the Platform only for the transaction concerned and not to send unsolicited marketing.

## 7. Checks

Approved Sellers' ads, like all ads, go live without waiting for approval and pass automatic (AI-assisted) checks; ads that may breach the rules can be taken into review. The gold badge does not exempt ads from checks.

## 8. Suspension and Termination of the Status

1. The Operator may suspend or withdraw Approved Seller status, and close the account if necessary, in the event of a breach of obligations, numerous justified complaints, false information or documents, the business ceasing to trade or conduct damaging the Platform's reputation.
2. The Approved Seller may give up the status at any time by writing to destek@ucuzuburada.com; the account then continues as an individual account.
3. To protect users, deletion of an Approved Seller account is subject to the Operator's approval. Where there is no open dispute or complaint, the Operator decides on the request within 7 days at the latest.

## 9. Liability

The Operator is not a party to transactions between the Approved Seller and users. The Approved Seller is personally liable for all claims, penalties and damages arising from their ads and transactions and agrees to meet any claims brought against the Operator for that reason. The gold badge does not mean that the Operator guarantees the Approved Seller's products or services.

## 10. Personal Data

The information given in the application and the details of the authorised person are processed, in line with the Privacy Policy and the Personal Data Notice, to grant and maintain the status, to contact the Approved Seller, for on-site visits and cooperation talks, and to meet legal obligations. Only the details the Approved Seller chooses to show on their profile are public; the authorised person's full name, phone number and address given in the application are not published unless the Approved Seller chooses to show them.

## 11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement is governed by TRNC law. The Courts and Enforcement Offices of Lefkoşa (Nicosia) have jurisdiction over disputes.
