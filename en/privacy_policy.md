# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how your personal data are collected, used, shared and protected when you use the ucuzuburada.com website and the Ucuzu Burada mobile applications (the "Platform"). The data controller is [personal detail; on the site] ("Operator", "we"). Your personal data are processed in accordance with the TRNC Personal Data Protection Law and related legislation. Details of your legal rights are set out in the Personal Data Notice.

## 1. Data We Collect

### 1.1 Information you provide

- **Account information:** name or business name, e-mail address, phone number, profile photo, password (stored irreversibly encrypted).
- **Ad information:** title, description, price, category-specific details, photos and videos, ad location and the contact number you choose to show.
- **Messages:** text, voice, images, files and offers you send in in-app messaging.
- **Profile verification and Approved Seller application:** the business's category, name, address, contact phone number and website (if any), the authorised person's full name and, if requested, the identity or business documents you upload. This information is stored in a non-public area and is viewed only by authorised staff for the review and for contacting you.
- **Job applications:** the information and CV file you share when you apply to a job ad.
- **Support and complaint records:** requests and complaints you send us and related correspondence.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically

- **Device and usage information:** device model, operating system, app version, language, IP address, access times, ads you view and favourite, search and filter preferences.
- **Location:** if you grant location permission, approximate or precise location to show nearby ads and make choosing an ad location easier. Location permission is optional and can be turned off in your device settings.
- **Notification token:** the key assigned to your device to send you message and ad notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
- **Crash reports:** when the app closes unexpectedly or an error occurs: the device model, operating system and app version, and the technical details of the error (Firebase Crashlytics). These reports are not linked to your account and are used only to fix errors.
- **Usage statistics:** events that do not identify you about which screens are opened and which features are used in the app (for example that a search was made and the word searched for, that an ad was viewed, the steps of posting an ad, sharing, adding to favourites); the device model, operating system, app version, language, an approximate location (country and city level) worked out from the IP address, and a random technical ID specific to this copy of the app on your device (Google Firebase Analytics). Personal details such as phone numbers, e-mail addresses and ad numbers are filtered out of the words searched for; your name, phone, e-mail, messages and account ID are not added to the statistics, and no advertising ID is collected. This data is not used to show ads or to follow you in other apps. Usage statistics are collected from every installation of the app.
- **Legal text acceptance record:** which version of the User Agreement and the Privacy Policy you accepted and when: your account, the version of the text you accepted, the time, whether the acceptance came from the app or the website, the app version, and your IP address in a shortened form (its last part removed). The record is kept so that, in case of a dispute, it can be proved which text you accepted and when, and it cannot be changed afterwards.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** see the Cookie Policy.

### 1.3 Information from third parties

- When you sign in with Google, Apple or Facebook, your name, e-mail and profile photo from that account (from Facebook only your public profile and e-mail address; nothing is ever posted on Facebook on your behalf);
- For in-app purchases, purchase verification information from Apple or Google (transaction number, product, date, refund status; your card details are not passed to us), together with your credit balance and credit history.

## 2. How We Use Data

- To create your account, verify your identity and phone number and keep your account secure;
- To publish your ads, translate them into other languages and show them in search results and relevant lists;
- To enable buyers and sellers to contact each other;
- Once paid services are offered, to provide them and verify purchases;
- To prevent, detect and investigate fraud, spam, fake accounts and rule breaches;
- To answer support requests and complaints;
- To improve the Platform, fix errors and produce statistics and usage analyses;
- With your consent, to send promotions and announcements;
- To record which legal text you accepted and when, so that it can be proved in a dispute;
- To comply with our legal obligations and duly issued requests from competent authorities.

## 3. Public Information

Your published ads and the public part of your profile (display name, profile photo, member since date, badge, reviews and active ads) can be seen by anyone using the Platform and by search engines. Your phone number is shown only if you choose to show it on an ad, and only to signed-in users. Your e-mail address is not shared with other users.

## 4. Sharing of Data

We do not sell your personal data. Data are shared only in the following cases and to the extent necessary:

- **Our service providers:** server and hosting services; Google Firebase for authentication, notifications, app crash reports and usage statistics; the Google Gemini AI service for translating ads, suggesting descriptions, automatic safety checks of ads and of profile and chat photos, smart search, the help assistant and, on request, translating chat messages (only the content being processed is sent); OpenStreetMap and/or Google Maps for maps; on the website, with your consent, Google Firebase Analytics and Microsoft Clarity for analytics; e-mail and SMS delivery services.
- **Payment providers:** Apple and Google, who handle in-app purchases.
- **Other users:** users you contact see your messages and the information you share in your ads. When you apply to a job ad, your application details and CV are sent to the advertiser.
- **Competent authorities:** upon duly issued requests of courts, prosecutors, the police and other competent public bodies, and where an offence is suspected.
- **Corporate transactions:** in the event of a merger, transfer or sale of assets, with the acquiring party, subject to confidentiality obligations.

## 5. International Transfers

Some of our service providers (Google, Apple, Meta, Microsoft and similar) may process data on servers outside the TRNC. Such transfers are made only as far as necessary to provide the service, under the providers' data protection commitments and adequate safeguards. By using the Platform and accepting this Policy you consent to these transfers.

## 6. Retention

- Account and ad information is kept while your account is open.
- When you delete your account, your profile and ads are removed from publication and the account is closed. Identity, transaction, message and security records are kept securely for the applicable statutory limitation periods for possible disputes, fraud investigations and requests from authorities, and are then deleted or anonymised.
- Purchase and accounting records are kept for the period required by tax and commercial legislation.
- Legal text acceptance records (which version you accepted and when) are kept as proof in possible disputes for the applicable statutory limitation periods, even if you delete your account; they are then deleted or anonymised.
- App crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.
- Usage statistics in the app are kept in Google Firebase Analytics for up to 14 months.

## 7. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS), irreversible password storage, access control and logging to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss and misuse. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Your Rights and Choices

- You can view and update your profile information in the Profile section of the app.
- You can delete your account with Delete Account at the bottom of Edit profile (the pencil on your card in the Profile tab) (see the Delete Your Account page).
- You can manage location, notification, camera and gallery permissions in your device settings.
- On the website, you can change your consent to analytics cookies in the cookie banner or your browser settings.
- You can send objections and deletion requests about the usage statistics in the app to kvkk@ucuzuburada.com.
- To exercise your legal rights regarding your personal data, write to kvkk@ucuzuburada.com (see the Personal Data Notice).

## 9. Children

The Platform is not intended for persons under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data of persons under 18. If you become aware of such a case, please tell us at kvkk@ucuzuburada.com.

## 10. Changes

We may update this Policy. The current version is published on this page; material changes are also announced by in-app notification or e-mail.

## 11. Contact

For privacy questions: kvkk@ucuzuburada.com
